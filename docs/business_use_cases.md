# Business Use Cases

## Product Performance

Identify top products by popularity, rating, value, and opportunity score.

## Hidden Gems

Find highly rated products with lower review volume that may deserve promotion.

## Category Intelligence

Compare category-level ratings, discounts, volume, and value scores.

## Customer Sentiment

Use Cortex sentiment to flag products with customer risk or unexpectedly negative reviews.

## Semantic Search and RAG

Retrieve relevant review context for natural-language product questions.

## Recommendations

Rank products with a combined signal from product health, opportunity, and sentiment.
